Help center

How do I start a chat?

Click “Open workspace”, type your message and press Enter or Send.

How do I start over?

Use “New chat” in the workspace to clear the conversation.

The assistant isn't replying

Allow the permission prompt when it appears, then try again. If it persists, wait a moment and resend.

Writing better prompts

Say who it's for, what you want, and the format. More detail gives better answers.
